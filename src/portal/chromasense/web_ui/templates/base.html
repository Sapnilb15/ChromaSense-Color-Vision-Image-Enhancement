{{ icon('lock') }} Session privacy

Pictures stay on this server for your session. ChromaSense does not diagnose color vision deficiency or give medical advice, and it does not show exactly what typical color vision sees.

How it works

  1. Drop a PNG or JPEG into the workspace or browse your files.
  2. Choose the Protan or Deutan profile.
  3. Set the severity from 0% to 100%.
  4. Enhance the image, then drag the divider or compare side by side.
  5. Download the enhanced PNG when an adjustment is available.

About ChromaSense

ChromaSense finds color pairs that may be hard to distinguish under your selected profile and adjusts targeted regions. Results describe simulated color separation.

Your anonymous session ends when you close or reload the page. Idle sessions expire after 30 minutes by default.
